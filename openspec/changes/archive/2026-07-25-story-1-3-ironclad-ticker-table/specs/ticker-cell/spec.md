## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Ticker Cell Formatting
The ticker cell SHALL display a company logo in a white disc, alongside the ticker symbol and a 1-sentence summary.

#### Scenario: Display ticker data
- **WHEN** the ticker cell is rendered with mock data
- **THEN** it shows a company logo inside a white disc
- **AND** it shows the symbol
- **AND** it shows a 1-sentence summary
