## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: AI Counter-Factual Scenarios
The system SHALL display AI-generated hypothetical scenarios comparing the user's actual execution against alternative decisions based on their documented rationale.

#### Scenario: Viewing Counter-Factuals
- **WHEN** the AI Post-Mortem is generated
- **THEN** it includes at least one counter-factual scenario (e.g., "Holding to Target")
- **AND** visually compares the hypothetical P/L against the actual realized P/L
